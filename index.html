How It Works

From understanding to preparation.

NYAYA.AI brings the work surrounding a legal matter into one connected workflow — helping you move from the initial case material to research, preparation and drafting, without repeatedly starting from scratch.

01 Understand

Build a clear picture of the matter.

Bring together the case documents, facts, parties, issues and other relevant information in one place. NYAYA.AI helps organize the matter into a structured understanding so you can quickly see what the case is about, what matters, and what requires attention.

02 Research

Find the law that matters to your case.

Research relevant Indian legal authorities around the specific issues in your matter. Instead of treating legal research as a disconnected search, NYAYA.AI keeps the research connected to the context of the case and provides access to the underlying authorities for your review.

03 Challenge

Don't only look for what supports you.

Strong preparation means understanding the arguments that may work against you. NYAYA.AI can deliberately look for contrary authorities, exceptions, limitations and opposing positions that could affect your argument — helping you identify potential weaknesses before they become surprises.

04 Prepare

Turn information into readiness.

Once the matter and research come together, NYAYA.AI helps you quickly revisit the important facts, issues, authorities and arguments. Use Brief Me and hearing preparation to regain context and organize what deserves attention before stepping into the next stage of the matter.

05 Draft

Move from research to a working draft.

Use the context already gathered around the matter to assist with creating first drafts. Instead of beginning with a blank page, NYAYA.AI can help turn your matter information and research into a starting point that you can review, refine and shape with your own legal judgment.

06 Remember

Come back to the matter without starting over.

Legal matters rarely end in a single session. NYAYA.AI keeps the matter's context connected so that when you return — days, weeks or months later — you can pick up where you left off rather than reconstructing the case from the beginning.
